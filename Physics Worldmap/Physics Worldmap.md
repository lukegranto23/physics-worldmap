## Candidate synthesis programs

> [!caution] Research status
> These are exploratory questions and benchmark designs, not claims of novelty, established equivalence, or confirmed new physics. A numerical pattern in a finite toy model is evidence about that implementation only until it survives controls, scaling tests, and comparison with prior work.

- **[[Synthesis Session 001 — Response-Preserving Coarse-Graining|Response-preserving coarse-graining]]** — asks when a reduced model can preserve selected response functions as well as stationary behavior. *Status: linear pilot completed; the wider four-system validation remains unfinished.*
- **[[Synthesis Session 002 — Holographic Coarse-Graining and the Memory of Spacetime|Memory diagnostics and entropy milestones]]** — asks whether carefully defined reduced-dynamics memory measures track independently defined entropy milestones in controlled finite models. A fixed-bipartition entropy maximum is not a black-hole Page time, and a selected trace-distance revival is not the optimized BLP measure. *Status: corrected exploratory diagnostic; gravitational test not implemented.*
- **[[Synthesis Session 003 — RG Flow as Information Geometry and the Shape of Scale|RG flow and information geometry]]** — asks when a multi-coupling RG flow admits a useful metric-gradient description. Alignment in a one-dimensional coupling space is automatic and therefore not a discriminating test. *Status: candidate program; multi-coupling benchmark needed.*
- **[[Synthesis Session 004 — The Geometry of Thermalization|State-space geometry and thermalization]]** — studies geometric diagnostics along reduced-state trajectories while keeping path length, endpoint distance, and information-backflow measures distinct. *Status: exploratory numerical geometry.*
- **[[Quantum Darwinism and Holographic Encoding]]** — compares redundant, classically accessible pointer records with operator-algebra reconstruction in holographic quantum error correction. *Status: structural comparison with explicit ways for the analogy to fail.*
- **[[Frustrated Systems and Computational Hardness]]** — proposes controlled comparisons among representation-dependent sign severity, variational difficulty, and bipartite entanglement structure. *Status: benchmark proposal; no universal correlation assumed.*

## The four-pass learning loop

1. **Orient:** locate an idea by field, scale, status, and neighboring ideas.
2. **Reconstruct:** derive it or explain why the approximation works.
3. **Confront:** connect it to an observation, experiment, or numerical test.
4. **Synthesize:** ask what structure transfers to a different field and what new prediction would distinguish the transfer from analogy.
