# Atomic Molecular and Optical Physics Map

> [!abstract]
> Atoms, molecules, light-matter interaction, precision control, ultracold matter, clocks, and quantum technology.

## Questions that organize the field

- How do electronic, vibrational, rotational, and nuclear degrees of freedom produce spectra?
- How can light coherently control matter and measure fundamental constants?
- How do dilute quantum gases realize clean many-body models?
- What limits precision, coherence, and scalability?

## Working principles

- Identify energy scales, selection rules, linewidths, and decoherence channels.
- Use symmetry and angular-momentum algebra to classify states.
- Separate coherent drive, spontaneous emission, and technical noise.
- Connect spectra to controlled Hamiltonians and calibrated metrology.

## Canonical mathematical handles

- **Hydrogen spectrum:** $E_n=-\mathrm{Ry}/n^2$ — Coulomb binding yields quantized levels.
- **Rabi oscillation:** $P_e(t)=\Omega^2\sin^2(\sqrt{\Omega^2+\Delta^2}t/2)/(\Omega^2+\Delta^2)$ — A coherent two-level drive transfers population.
- **Dipole coupling:** $H_I=-\mathbf d\cdot\mathbf E$ — Electric-dipole interaction drives optical transitions.
- **Doppler limit:** $T_D=\hbar\Gamma/(2k_B)$ — Linewidth sets a basic two-level laser-cooling scale.

## Concept atlas

| Concept | Level | Status | One-line orientation |
|---|---:|---|---|
| [[Hydrogen Atom]] | introductory | established | The Coulomb two-body problem yields orbitals labeled by principal, orbital, magnetic, and spin quantum numbers. |
| [[Atomic Orbitals]] | introductory | established | One-electron bound states have radial and angular structure classified by rotation symmetry and nodes. |
| [[Fine Structure]] | advanced | established | Relativistic kinetic, spin-orbit, and Darwin corrections split hydrogenic levels beyond the Schrodinger spectrum. |
| [[Hyperfine Structure]] | advanced | established | Coupling between electronic and nuclear multipole moments produces smaller spectral splittings. |
| [[Zeeman Effect]] | intermediate | established | Magnetic fields split atomic levels according to magnetic moments and angular-momentum coupling regimes. |
| [[Stark Effect]] | intermediate | established | Electric fields shift and mix atomic levels through permanent or induced dipole moments. |
| [[Atomic Selection Rules]] | intermediate | established | Symmetry and multipole structure constrain allowed transition matrix elements. |
| [[Spontaneous and Stimulated Emission]] | intermediate | established | Matter-light coupling produces absorption, stimulated emission, and vacuum-enabled spontaneous decay. |
| [[Two Level Systems]] | intermediate | established | A near-resonant isolated transition reduces to a driven effective spin one-half with detuning and Rabi frequency. |
| [[Rabi Oscillations]] | intermediate | established | A coherent resonant drive cycles population between two quantum levels. |
| [[Ramsey Interferometry]] | advanced | established | Two separated coherent pulses convert phase accumulation into high-resolution fringes. |
| [[Laser Physics]] | intermediate | established | Population inversion, stimulated emission, gain, feedback, and saturation produce coherent optical oscillation. |
| [[Laser Cooling]] | intermediate | established | Velocity-dependent photon scattering and recoil reduce atomic kinetic energy, with limits set by linewidth and recoil. |
| [[Magneto Optical Traps]] | intermediate | established | Laser cooling plus a magnetic-field gradient provides velocity and position-dependent forces that trap neutral atoms. |
| [[Optical Traps and Tweezers]] | intermediate | established | AC Stark shifts create conservative potentials for atoms, molecules, and dielectric particles. |
| [[Bose Einstein Condensation]] | intermediate | established | Below a critical temperature, bosons macroscopically occupy a coherent mode. |
| [[Degenerate Fermi Gases]] | intermediate | established | Ultracold fermions fill momentum states up to a Fermi surface and realize tunable many-body regimes. |
| [[Feshbach Resonances]] | advanced | established | A magnetic field tunes scattering through a coupled molecular state, controlling the interaction strength. |
| [[Optical Lattices]] | advanced | established | Standing waves create periodic potentials that emulate lattice models with tunable tunneling and interactions. |
| [[Ion Traps]] | advanced | established | Electric and magnetic fields confine charged particles for precision spectroscopy, clocks, and quantum logic. |
| [[Molecular Rotation and Vibration]] | intermediate | established | Molecular spectra separate approximately into electronic, vibrational, and rotational energy scales. |
| [[Born Oppenheimer Approximation]] | advanced | established | Slow nuclei and fast electrons are approximately separated, generating molecular potential-energy surfaces. |
| [[Precision Spectroscopy]] | intermediate | active | Stable frequencies and controlled systematics turn narrow transitions into tests of constants and fundamental symmetries. |
| [[Atomic Clocks]] | advanced | active | A clock locks an oscillator to an atomic transition, with accuracy set by systematic shifts and stability by signal-to-noise. |
| [[Cavity Quantum Electrodynamics]] | advanced | active | Confined light modes interact strongly and coherently with quantum emitters when coupling exceeds losses. |
| [[Strong Field and Attosecond Physics]] | advanced | active | Intense ultrashort fields drive tunneling ionization, recollision, high harmonics, and subcycle electron motion. |
