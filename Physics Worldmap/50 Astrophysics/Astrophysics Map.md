# Astrophysics Map

> [!abstract]
> Stars, compact objects, galaxies, interstellar matter, high-energy sources, and multimessenger observations.

## Questions that organize the field

- How do gravity, radiation, nuclear reactions, fields, and fluids build cosmic objects?
- How do stars and galaxies form, evolve, and enrich the universe?
- What are the engines of transients, jets, and cosmic rays?
- What can photons, particles, and gravitational waves jointly reveal?

## Working principles

- Convert observables through a forward model with selection effects and distance uncertainty.
- Compare dynamical, thermal, nuclear, radiative, and transport timescales.
- Hydrostatic balance and virial reasoning provide first estimates.
- Multimessenger consistency reduces degeneracy.

## Canonical mathematical handles

- **Hydrostatic equilibrium:** $dP/dr=-Gm(r)\rho/r^2$ — Pressure gradients support stars against gravity.
- **Luminosity transport:** $dL/dr=4\pi r^2\rho\epsilon$ — Local energy generation accumulates outward.
- **Virial theorem:** $2K+U=0$ — Bound self-gravitating systems relate kinetic and potential energy.
- **Eddington luminosity:** $L_E=4\pi GMm_pc/\sigma_T$ — Radiation force balances gravity for ionized gas.
