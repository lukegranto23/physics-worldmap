# Geophysics and Climate Map

> [!abstract]
> Earth and planetary interiors, surfaces, atmospheres, oceans, magnetism, climate, and coupled natural hazards.

## Questions that organize the field

- How do planetary interiors, surfaces, oceans, atmospheres, and biospheres exchange energy and matter?
- What controls earthquakes, volcanism, dynamos, and long-term tectonics?
- How can weather, climate, and hazards be predicted under chaos and sparse observation?
- Which feedbacks set planetary habitability?

## Working principles

- Earth systems are rotating, stratified, turbulent, forced, dissipative, and multiscale.
- Conservation laws interact with uncertain constitutive relations and incomplete observations.
- Separate weather initial-value prediction from climate statistical response.
- Validate models across process scales, paleorecords, and modern observations.

## Canonical mathematical handles

- **Geostrophic balance:** $f\hat z\times\mathbf u=-\nabla_h p/\rho$ — Coriolis and pressure-gradient forces balance at large scale.
- **Energy balance climate:** $C\,dT/dt=S(1-\alpha)/4-\mathrm{OLR}(T)$ — Absorbed sunlight and outgoing radiation set global energy.
- **Seismic wave speeds:** $v_P=\sqrt{(K+4\mu/3)/\rho},\ v_S=\sqrt{\mu/\rho}$ — Elastic properties control body-wave propagation.
- **Dynamo induction:** $\partial_t\mathbf B=\nabla\times(\mathbf u\times\mathbf B)+\eta\nabla^2\mathbf B$ — Conducting flow generates and diffuses magnetic field.

| [[Volcanology]] | intermediate | active | Magma generation, ascent, crystallization, gas exsolution, and conduit dynamics control eruptions. |
| [[Gravity and Geodesy]] | intermediate | established | Precise shape, gravity, position, and deformation measurements reveal mass redistribution and tectonic motion. |
| [[Geomagnetism and the Geodynamo]] | advanced | active | Convecting conducting liquid in Earth's rotating outer core sustains a self-excited magnetic field. |
| [[Ocean Circulation]] | intermediate | active | Wind, buoyancy, rotation, mixing, and basin geometry drive currents that redistribute heat, carbon, and nutrients. |
| [[Atmospheric Dynamics]] | intermediate | established | Stratified rotating compressible flow transports momentum, heat, water, and trace gases across scales. |
