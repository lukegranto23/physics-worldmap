# Climate Sensitivity

> [!summary] Core idea
> Equilibrium warming from doubled carbon dioxide summarizes combined fast feedback response with a probability distribution.

> [!caution] Orientation status
> This is a compact orientation note and has not yet been source-audited. The epistemic label describes the standing of the underlying physics in its stated regime; it does not certify every sentence or equation in this note.

## Mathematical handle

$\mathrm{ECS}=F_{2xCO2}/\lambda$

Here $\lambda>0$ is the net restoring-feedback parameter in the convention $\Delta N=F-\lambda\Delta T$. Other literature defines a signed feedback parameter with the opposite sign, so the convention must always be stated.
