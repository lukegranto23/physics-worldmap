# Relativity and Gravitation Map

> [!abstract]
> Spacetime, causal geometry, relativistic dynamics, gravitation, compact objects, and gravitational waves.

## Questions that organize the field

- What observations are invariant across observers and coordinate systems?
- How does stress-energy shape spacetime geometry?
- When do horizons, singularities, and gravitational radiation form?
- Where and why do general relativity and quantum theory fail to coexist?

## Working principles

- Physical predictions are coordinate-invariant even when components are not.
- Local freely falling frames recover special relativity.
- Geometry and matter are coupled; neither is a fixed background in general relativity.
- Always identify approximation regime: weak field, post-Newtonian, perturbative, or fully nonlinear.

## Canonical mathematical handles

- **Interval:** $ds^2=g_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu dx^\nu$ — The metric defines causal and geometric structure.
- **Geodesic:** $d^2x^\mu/d\tau^2+\Gamma^\mu_{\alpha\beta}u^\alpha u^\beta=0$ — Free fall follows extremal proper-time paths.
- **Einstein equation:** $G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}=8\pi G T_{\mu\nu}/c^4$ — Curvature is sourced by stress-energy.
- **Geodesic deviation:** $D^2\xi^\mu/D\tau^2=-R^\mu{}_{\nu\alpha\beta}u^\nu\xi^\alpha u^\beta$ — Curvature produces tidal acceleration.

| [[Relativistic Energy Momentum]] | introductory | established | Energy and momentum form a four-vector with an invariant mass shell relation. |
| [[Relativistic Dynamics]] | intermediate | established | Four-force changes four-momentum while maintaining the mass-shell constraint. |
| [[Equivalence Principle]] | introductory | established | Locally, freely falling experiments cannot distinguish a uniform gravitational field from acceleration, motivating geometric gravity. |
| [[Metric and Proper Time]] | intermediate | established | The metric determines measured durations, distances, angles, volumes, and light cones. |
| [[Covariant Derivatives and Connection]] | advanced | established | A connection compares tensors at nearby points and defines parallel transport and geodesics. |
| [[Curvature]] | advanced | established | The Riemann tensor measures noncommuting covariant derivatives and tidal geometry. |
| [[Einstein Field Equations]] | advanced | established | The Einstein tensor and cosmological term are related to stress-energy, with local covariant conservation built in. |
| [[Stress Energy Tensor]] | advanced | established | Stress-energy packages local energy density, momentum density, pressure, and shear as the source of gravity. |
| [[Schwarzschild Spacetime]] | intermediate | established | The exterior of an isolated nonrotating spherical mass is described by the Schwarzschild solution. |
| [[Black Holes]] | advanced | established | A black hole contains a region causally disconnected from future null infinity and is characterized macroscopically by mass, spin, and charge in idealized GR. |
| [[Kerr Spacetime]] | advanced | established | The stationary rotating vacuum black-hole solution exhibits frame dragging, an ergoregion, and inner and outer horizons. |
| [[Gravitational Redshift]] | intermediate | established | Clock rates and photon frequencies differ between gravitational potentials or stationary metric positions. |
| [[Gravitational Lensing]] | intermediate | established | Spacetime curvature deflects light, producing multiple images, shear, magnification, and time delays. |
| [[Frame Dragging]] | advanced | established | Rotating mass drags local inertial frames, producing Lense-Thirring precession. |
| [[Post Newtonian Expansion]] | advanced | established | Weak-field slow-motion gravity is expanded systematically in powers of $v/c$ and gravitational potential. |
| [[Gravitational Waves]] | intermediate | established | Accelerating nonspherical mass-energy distributions emit propagating transverse spacetime strain. |
| [[Binary Inspiral]] | advanced | established | Energy loss to gravitational radiation shrinks compact binaries and chirps their orbital frequency. |
| [[Black Hole Thermodynamics]] | advanced | established | Horizon area, surface gravity, mass, angular momentum, and charge obey laws analogous to thermodynamics. |
| [[Hawking Radiation]] | advanced | mixed | Semiclassical QFT predicts $T_H=\hbar\kappa/(2\pi c k_B)$; this is model-conditionally established but astrophysical Hawking radiation has not been directly detected. |
| [[Singularities and Cosmic Censorship]] | advanced | open | Geodesic incompleteness occurs under broad conditions; whether generic singularities are hidden by horizons remains unresolved. |
| [[Quantum Gravity]] | advanced | open | A quantum theory of dynamical spacetime is required where curvature and quantum fluctuations are simultaneously strong. |
| [[Holographic Principle]] | advanced | conjectural | Black-hole entropy and covariant bounds motivate the proposal that quantum-gravity information content scales with boundary area; its general scope is unproved. |
| [[AdS-CFT Correspondence]] | advanced | conjectural | A deeply tested conjecture equates particular string theories in anti-de Sitter space with boundary conformal field theories; most calculations control special limits or protected sectors. |
| [[Black Hole Information Problem]] | advanced | open | Whether and how information is preserved during black hole evaporation remains the sharpest constraint on any theory of quantum gravity. |
