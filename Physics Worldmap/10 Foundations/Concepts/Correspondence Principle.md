# Correspondence Principle

> [!summary] Core idea
> In a regime where two descriptions overlap, a more general theory should recover the empirically successful predictions of the older effective description to its tested accuracy—not necessarily its ontology or every formal expression.

> [!caution] Orientation status
> This is a compact orientation note and has not yet been source-audited. The epistemic label describes the standing of the underlying physics in its stated regime; it does not certify every sentence or equation in this note.

## Mathematical handle

$S/\hbar\gg1$ or $v/c\ll1$ are common correspondence regimes

Correspondence can require coarse-graining, restricted observables, decoherence, or matched asymptotic limits rather than simply setting a constant to zero or infinity. Limits may be singular or noncommuting, so the recovered predictions, accuracy, and assumptions must be stated explicitly.
