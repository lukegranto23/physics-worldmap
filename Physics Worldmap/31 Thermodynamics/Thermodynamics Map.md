# Thermodynamics Map

> [!abstract]
> Macroscopic constraints on energy, entropy, equilibrium, work, heat, matter, and irreversibility.

## Questions that organize the field

- Which state variables suffice at a chosen macroscopic resolution?
- What transformations are possible, efficient, stable, or irreversible?
- How do thermodynamic laws emerge statistically?
- How do thermodynamic ideas extend to small, active, information-processing, and quantum systems?

## Working principles

- State functions depend on endpoints; heat and work depend on process.
- Choose the thermodynamic potential matched to controlled variables.
- Stability is encoded by convexity and response functions.
- Entropy production marks irreversibility, but requires a specified coarse-graining.

## Canonical mathematical handles

- **First law:** $dU=\delta Q-\delta W$ — Energy accounting separates heat and work transfers.
- **Fundamental relation:** $dU=T\,dS-p\,dV+\mu\,dN$ — Conjugate variables organize equilibrium changes.
- **Clausius:** $\Delta S\ge\int\delta Q/T$ — Total entropy does not decrease for an isolated macroscopic system.
- **Gibbs free energy:** $G=U-TS+pV$ — At fixed T and p, equilibrium minimizes G.
