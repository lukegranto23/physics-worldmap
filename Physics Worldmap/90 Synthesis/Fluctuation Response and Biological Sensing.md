# Fluctuation Response and Biological Sensing

At equilibrium, fluctuation-dissipation relations tie spontaneous noise to linear response. Living sensors consume chemical free energy, so deviations from the equilibrium relation can reveal hidden dissipation and active feedback.

## Established core

[[Fluctuation Dissipation Theorem]] predicts response from equilibrium correlations under specified assumptions. [[Sensory Adaptation]] and biochemical networks commonly violate those assumptions through driven cycles.

## Connection

Measure response and spontaneous fluctuations over frequency. Their mismatch is not merely “noise”; under suitable models it constrains entropy production or hidden active processes.

## Testable direction

Compare information gained about a controlled input with frequency-resolved FDT violation and chemical work. The conjectural step is that spectral structure can yield a tighter task-specific bound than total dissipation alone.
