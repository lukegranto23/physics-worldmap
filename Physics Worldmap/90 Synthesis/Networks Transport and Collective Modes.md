# Networks Transport and Collective Modes

Mass-spring systems, electrical circuits, diffusion networks, Markov chains, and synchronization models all use graph operators. The graph Laplacian's eigenvectors are collective modes; small eigenvalues expose slow mixing, weakly connected regions, or soft deformation.

## Shared operator

For a weighted graph, $L=D-A$. Depending on context:

- $\dot x=-Lx$ describes diffusion or consensus;
- $M\ddot x+Lx=0$ describes coupled oscillations;
- circuit admittance and elastic stiffness generalize the same incidence structure.

## Limits

The state space, conservation law, boundary conditions, sign structure, nonlinearity, and noise determine the physics. Spectral similarity alone is not mechanism.

## Use

This bridge suggests common tools for sensor placement, controllability, coarse-graining, and mode localization across engineered, biological, and material networks.
