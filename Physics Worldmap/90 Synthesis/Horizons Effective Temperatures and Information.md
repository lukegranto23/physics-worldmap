# Horizons Effective Temperatures and Information

Accelerated observers, black-hole horizons, expanding spacetimes, and analogue media can mix positive- and negative-frequency modes so restricted observers describe thermal populations.

## Model-conditional established pieces

- [[Hawking Radiation]] is a robust theoretical result of semiclassical quantum field theory on a prescribed black-hole background, but Hawking radiation from an astrophysical black hole has not been directly observed;
- the laws of [[Black Hole Thermodynamics]] are well-established theoretical relations in classical and semiclassical gravity, while the statistical microstates behind horizon entropy remain open;
- laboratory analogue systems have observed or constrained mode-conversion and effective-horizon phenomena in specific media, not gravitational Hawking radiation itself.

## What analogues do not establish

They do not reproduce Einstein dynamics, quantum gravitational microstates, or black-hole backreaction automatically. They can test the robustness of kinematic mode-conversion mechanisms.

## Open bridge

Why gravitational entropy scales with area and how information is preserved through full evaporation remain unresolved.
