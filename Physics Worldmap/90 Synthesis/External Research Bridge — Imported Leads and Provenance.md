# External Research Bridge — Imported Leads and Provenance

External notes and computational projects may suggest leads, but their content is not automatically evidence in this portable vault. Earlier references to unbundled implementations were not independently reproduced and have been withdrawn as support.

## Promotion checklist

Before importing a result:

1. Record the primary source, author, date, license, and exact version.
2. Bundle or link the actual method and data with permission.
3. Specify model, units, initial conditions, numerical precision, and random seed.
4. Reproduce analytic anchors and independent checks.
5. Separate finite-size observations from extrapolations and conjectures.
6. Record failed tests and corrections alongside successful runs.
7. Make the resulting note understandable without access to a private external workspace.

## Leads, not results

Frustrated-spin diagonalization, variational-state benchmarks, and code-reconstruction experiments are possible future inputs. None is certified by an external project name or an earlier conversational assertion.

The currently bundled exploratory labs are documented in [[Computational Lab Index]]. Their interpretation is limited by the corresponding synthesis sessions. Archived plots produced before the precision pass are not evidence for the revised hypotheses.
