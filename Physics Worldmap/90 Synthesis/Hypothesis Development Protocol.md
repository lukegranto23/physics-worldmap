# Hypothesis Development Protocol

## One-page hypothesis card

1. **Question:** one sentence, one observable target.
2. **Baseline:** current best model and its quantitative prediction.
3. **Gap:** anomaly, unexplained structure, or new regime—not a vague dissatisfaction.
4. **Minimal change:** smallest new degree of freedom, interaction, boundary condition, or inference assumption.
5. **Consistency:** dimensions, symmetries, conservation, stability, causality, unitarity or probability, known limits.
6. **Derived prediction:** calculate a sign, scaling, spectrum, correlation, threshold, or null.
7. **Discriminator:** where does it differ from the baseline and alternatives?
8. **Feasibility:** instrument, dataset, or simulation needed.
9. **Falsifier:** outcome that would lower confidence.
10. **Prior art:** closest known literature and why this is or is not distinct.

## Promotion states

`spark → analogy → mapped mechanism → consistent toy model → quantitative prediction → test proposal → evidence`

Never skip the middle stages in the written record.
