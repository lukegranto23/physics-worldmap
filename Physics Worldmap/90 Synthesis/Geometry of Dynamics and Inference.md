# Geometry of Dynamics and Inference

Mechanics uses geometry to encode constraints and invariant motion; statistics uses geometry to encode distinguishability between probability distributions. Both replace coordinate-dependent components with invariant distances, curvatures, or flows.

## Established structures

- symplectic geometry organizes [[Hamiltonian Mechanics]];
- Riemannian geometry organizes [[Relativity and Gravitation Map]];
- Fisher information defines a local metric on model distributions;
- optimal transport gives geometry to probability rearrangement.

## Warning

A shared word such as “curvature” does not imply the same physical entity. Spacetime curvature is gravitational geometry; statistical curvature describes a family of distributions.

## Research use

Use information geometry to diagnose parameter degeneracy and experiment design, then ask whether the physical dynamics itself is a gradient, Hamiltonian, or mixed flow on the same state manifold.
