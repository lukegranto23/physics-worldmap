# Canonical Textbook Guide

These are bibliographic recommendations, not included copies. Edition choice is usually secondary to working the problems.

| Area | Introductory | Advanced or graduate |
|---|---|---|
| mechanics | Taylor; Kleppner and Kolenkow | Goldstein; Arnold |
| electromagnetism | Griffiths; Purcell and Morin | Jackson; Landau and Lifshitz |
| quantum | Griffiths and Schroeter; Shankar | Sakurai and Napolitano; Cohen-Tannoudji |
| thermodynamics and statistical physics | Schroeder; Kittel and Kroemer | Pathria and Beale; Kardar |
| mathematical methods | Boas; Riley Hobson and Bence | Arfken Weber and Harris; Nakahara for geometry |
| relativity | Taylor and Wheeler; Hartle | Carroll; Wald |
| field theory and particles | Zee; Thomson | Peskin and Schroeder; Weinberg; Schwartz |
| condensed matter | Kittel; Simon | Ashcroft and Mermin; Altland and Simons; Fradkin |
| fluids | Acheson; Kundu Cohen and Dowling | Landau and Lifshitz; Pope for turbulence |
| plasma | Chen | Goldston and Rutherford; Stix |
| astrophysics | Carroll and Ostlie | Rybicki and Lightman; Binney and Tremaine |
| cosmology | Ryden | Dodelson and Schmidt; Mukhanov |
| nuclear | Krane | Ring and Schuck; modern EFT reviews |
| biophysics | Phillips et al.; Nelson | Dill and Bromberg; specialized reviews |
| computation | Newman; Pang | Press et al. as reference, plus method-specific texts |
| experiment and data | Hughes and Hase; Taylor | Cowan; Lyons; collaboration statistics notes |

Use the map notes to decide prerequisites, then consult current syllabi and reviews because emphasis and notation differ.
