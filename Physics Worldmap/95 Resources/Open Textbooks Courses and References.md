# Open Textbooks Courses and References

This is a legal study index, not a mirror of copyrighted books.

Companions: [[Canonical Textbook Guide]] · [[Architecture and Completeness Audit]] · [[Vault Health Report]]

## Broad foundations

- [The Feynman Lectures on Physics](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/) — broad conceptual reference.
- [OpenStax University Physics](https://openstax.org/details/books/university-physics-volume-1) — calculus-based undergraduate sequence.
- [MIT OpenCourseWare Physics](https://ocw.mit.edu/search/?d=Physics) — courses, notes, assignments, and some solutions.
- [Physics LibreTexts](https://phys.libretexts.org/) — broad open educational library; quality and depth vary by page.

## Specialist living references

- [Particle Data Group Review of Particle Physics](https://pdg.lbl.gov/) — particle properties, formulas, and reviews.
- [NIST Reference on Constants, Units, and Uncertainty](https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/) — constants and measurement reference.
- [CODATA](https://codata.org/) — international data standards and fundamental constants work.
- [NASA Astrophysics Data System](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/) — literature index for astronomy and physics.
- [INSPIRE](https://inspirehep.net/) — literature, data, and author records for high-energy physics.
- [Living Reviews in Relativity](https://www.springer.com/journal/41114) — long-form review articles.

## Computation and data

- [NumPy documentation](https://numpy.org/doc/) and [SciPy documentation](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/) — numerical foundations.
- [Matplotlib documentation](https://matplotlib.org/stable/) — scientific visualization.
- [CERN Open Data Portal](https://opendata.cern.ch/) — collider datasets and educational analyses.
- [LIGO Open Science Center](https://gwosc.org/) — gravitational-wave data and tutorials.
- [NASA HEASARC](https://heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov/) — high-energy astrophysics archives.

## How to select a textbook

Use one primary text for a coherent course, one alternative explanation, a problem source, and a living review for frontier status. Write your own derivations and cite page or section rather than copying prose.
