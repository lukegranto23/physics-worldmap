# Nuclear Physics Map

> [!abstract]
> Nuclei, nucleons, nuclear forces, reactions, decays, dense matter, and element formation.

## Questions that organize the field

- How does QCD produce nuclear forces and many-body structure?
- Where are the limits of nuclear existence and what are exotic nuclei like?
- What is the equation of state of neutron-rich matter?
- How are elements synthesized in stars, explosions, and mergers?

## Working principles

- Nuclear descriptions are effective and scale-dependent.
- Symmetry, separation energies, and collective modes organize spectra.
- Reactions require conservation laws, channel thresholds, and cross sections.
- Connect laboratory nuclei to astrophysical environments with uncertainty propagation.

## Canonical mathematical handles

- **Binding energy:** $B=[Zm_p+Nm_n-M(A,Z)]c^2$ — Mass defect measures nuclear binding.
- **Radioactive decay:** $N(t)=N_0e^{-\lambda t}$ — Independent unstable nuclei decay exponentially.
- **Cross section:** $R=\Phi n_t\sigma$ — Reaction rate links flux, targets, and interaction probability.
- **Semiempirical mass:** $B=a_vA-a_sA^{2/3}-a_cZ^2A^{-1/3}-a_a(A-2Z)^2/A+\delta$ — Bulk and pairing terms capture mass systematics.

|---|---:|---|---|
| [[Nuclear Composition and Isotopes]] | introductory | established | Nuclei contain protons and neutrons; isotopes share proton number but differ in neutron number and stability. |
| [[Nuclear Size and Density]] | introductory | established | Nuclear radii scale approximately as $A^{1/3}$, implying nearly constant interior density. |
| [[Nuclear Binding Energy]] | introductory | established | Binding energy is the mass-energy difference between a nucleus and its separated nucleons. |
| [[Nuclear Force]] | advanced | active | The residual strong interaction is short-ranged, spin- and isospin-dependent, noncentral, and described at low energy by effective interactions. |
| [[Semi Empirical Mass Formula]] | intermediate | effective | A liquid-drop-inspired formula captures volume, surface, Coulomb, asymmetry, and pairing contributions to masses. |
| [[Nuclear Shell Model]] | advanced | established | Nucleons occupy mean-field orbitals with spin-orbit splitting; residual interactions build spectra and magic numbers. |
| [[Collective Nuclear Model]] | advanced | established | Nuclei can rotate and vibrate collectively, bridging shell and liquid-drop behavior. |
| [[Nuclear Spin and Moments]] | intermediate | established | Nuclear angular momentum, parity, magnetic dipole, and electric quadrupole moments probe internal structure. |
| [[Radioactive Decay]] | introductory | established | Unstable nuclei transform probabilistically with an approximately constant hazard for isolated identical nuclei. |
| [[Alpha Decay]] | intermediate | established | A preformed alpha cluster tunnels through the Coulomb barrier, explaining strong lifetime-energy dependence. |
| [[Beta Decay]] | intermediate | established | Weak interaction converts neutrons and protons while emitting leptons and obeying flavor and energy-momentum constraints. |
| [[Gamma Decay]] | intermediate | established | Excited nuclei emit photons through multipole transitions constrained by angular momentum and parity selection rules. |
| [[Fission]] | introductory | established | A heavy nucleus can split through deformation and barrier penetration, releasing binding energy and neutrons. |
| [[Fusion]] | introductory | established | Light nuclei combine and release binding energy after penetrating or overcoming Coulomb repulsion. |
| [[Nuclear Reactions]] | intermediate | established | Entrance and exit channels are described by thresholds, resonances, angular distributions, and cross sections. |
| [[Compound Nucleus]] | advanced | effective | An incident particle may form a long-lived equilibrated intermediate whose decay is approximately independent of formation details. |
| [[Direct Nuclear Reactions]] | advanced | established | Fast reactions probe specific nuclear degrees of freedom without full equilibration. |
| [[Effective Field Theory for Nuclei]] | advanced | active | Low-energy nuclear observables are expanded in momenta over a breakdown scale with symmetry-constrained operators. |
| [[Nuclear Many Body Methods]] | advanced | active | Configuration interaction, coupled cluster, Monte Carlo, density functionals, and tensor networks approximate interacting nuclei. |
| [[Neutron Rich and Exotic Nuclei]] | advanced | active | Weak binding, halos, skins, and altered shell structure appear far from stability. |
| [[Nuclear Matter]] | advanced | active | Infinite nucleonic matter provides an idealized equation of state relevant to nuclei and compact stars. |
| [[Neutrinoless Double Beta Decay]] | advanced | open | A hypothetical lepton-number-violating decay would show neutrinos are Majorana particles under standard interpretations. |
| [[Nucleosynthesis]] | advanced | active | Reaction networks build nuclei in the early universe, stars, explosions, and mergers. |
