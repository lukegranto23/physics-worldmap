# Beta Decay

> [!summary] Core idea
> Charged-current weak interactions change nuclear charge through beta-minus decay, beta-plus decay, or electron capture, with the accompanying electron-flavor neutrino or antineutrino required by the Standard Model's lepton-number bookkeeping and energy–momentum conservation.

> [!caution] Orientation status
> This is a compact orientation note and has not yet been source-audited. The epistemic label describes the standing of the underlying physics in its stated regime; it does not certify every sentence or equation in this note.

## Mathematical handle

$n\to p+e^-+\bar\nu_e$ (free-neutron or nuclear $\beta^-$ decay)

At quark level, $d\to u+W^-$ underlies beta-minus decay; the crossed processes describe beta-plus decay and electron capture. Whether a nuclear channel occurs depends on the parent and daughter mass-energy difference, nuclear matrix elements, angular momentum, and selection rules—not merely on the presence of a neutron or proton.
