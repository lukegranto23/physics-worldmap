# Problem Solving Protocol

1. **Restate:** What is asked, observed, controlled, and unknown?
2. **Sketch:** Draw geometry, causal order, system boundary, and sign conventions.
3. **Inventory:** List variables with dimensions and plausible scales.
4. **Regime:** Identify small or large dimensionless parameters.
5. **Principles:** Choose conservation laws, symmetry, constitutive laws, and state equations.
6. **Represent:** Select coordinates, basis, gauge, ensemble, or numerical discretization.
7. **Estimate:** Predict sign and order of magnitude before calculation.
8. **Solve:** Keep symbolic structure until late.
9. **Check:** units, limits, symmetry, conservation, positivity, causality, and special cases.
10. **Interpret:** State what the answer means operationally and where it fails.
11. **Verify:** compare with an independent method, simulation, or data if risk warrants.

## Emergency questions when stuck

- What would happen if a parameter were zero or infinite?
- Can the system be decomposed into normal modes or conserved sectors?
- Is there a hidden symmetry or useful frame?
- Is the problem linear after a transform?
- Does energy, probability, charge, or flux have somewhere to go?
- Is an apparent paradox mixing frames, ensembles, or coarse-grainings?
