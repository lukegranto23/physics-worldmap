# Recall Question Bank

## Foundations

1. Distinguish a theory, model, approximation, interpretation, and observable.
2. Give three ways a mathematically consistent model can fail physically.
3. Explain why dimensionless constants are more invariant than dimensional constants.

## Dynamics

1. Derive a conservation law from a continuous symmetry.
2. Explain phase space and why Hamiltonian flow preserves its volume.
3. Distinguish instability, resonance, chaos, and stochastic forcing.

## Fields and waves

1. Derive a wave equation from a local restoring law.
2. Explain phase velocity versus group velocity with a dispersive example.
3. State Maxwell's equations in words and differential form.

## Quantum and thermal

1. Distinguish a superposition from a mixture experimentally.
2. Derive a canonical distribution for a small system coupled to a large bath.
3. Explain why entropy increase does not contradict reversible microscopic equations.
4. Explain what renormalization group flow adds beyond “average over details.”

## Relativity and cosmos

1. Explain proper time without saying “moving clocks run slow.”
2. Distinguish coordinate singularities from curvature singularities.
3. Derive the qualitative expansion scaling for matter and radiation.
4. Name independent evidence for dark matter and what each actually establishes.

## Research hygiene

1. What observable would falsify your favored explanation?
2. Which nuisance parameter could imitate the effect?
3. Which limiting theory must be recovered?
4. Has the same mathematical structure already appeared in a neighboring field?
