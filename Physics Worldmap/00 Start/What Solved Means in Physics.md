# What Solved Means in Physics

“Solved” is not one state. A vault that uses the word without a qualifier will eventually confuse mathematical tractability, experimental confidence, mechanistic understanding, and engineering maturity.

## Solution-state vocabulary

| State | Meaning | Example pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **exactly solved model** | a specified idealized model has a closed or complete mathematical solution | two-body Newtonian gravity; quantum harmonic oscillator |
| **asymptotically controlled** | a systematic expansion has a declared small parameter and error order | post-Newtonian gravity; low-energy effective field theory |
| **numerically controlled** | convergence, stability, and uncertainty are demonstrated for a specified calculation | a lattice or PDE result after continuum/grid and finite-volume audits |
| **empirically established** | independent observations support a prediction within a stated domain | gravitational waves; neutrino oscillations |
| **effectively modeled** | a phenomenological or coarse theory predicts well without complete microscopic derivation | many constitutive laws and turbulence closures |
| **mechanistically explained** | relevant degrees of freedom and causal pathway are identified and tested | band theory for broad classes of crystalline electronic behavior |
| **engineered** | a phenomenon is reliable, scalable, safe, maintainable, and economically usable for a purpose | a commercial technology, not merely a laboratory milestone |

These labels can coexist. A model may be mathematically exact but physically idealized; a phenomenon may be empirically undeniable but microscopically unexplained.

## Examples of mixed status

### Three-body gravity

- governing Newtonian model: established in its regime;
- generic analytic closed form: unavailable;
- numerical trajectory over finite time: often controlled;
- very long-term prediction: may be limited by chaos and uncertain initial data.

### Turbulence

- conservation laws and Navier–Stokes model: established for continuum Newtonian fluids;
- all-scale prediction in realistic flows: computationally and empirically difficult;
- universal closure theory: open;
- mathematical regularity in three dimensions: open.

### Dark matter

- extra gravitating component in the standard cosmological account: supported by multiple observations;
- microscopic identity: open;
- any particular particle candidate: constrained, not established.

### Fusion energy

- nuclear reaction energetics: established;
- high-performance burning plasma: experimentally demonstrated in specific regimes;
- reliable net-electric, maintainable, economical plant: engineering frontier.

### Quantum measurement

- outcome probabilities and laboratory predictions: extraordinarily well established;
- Bell-inequality violations: established under explicit assumptions;
- unique ontology or interpretation: not experimentally selected by standard tests.

## Promotion rule

Before changing a status:

1. name the exact question and regime;
2. state which solution state is claimed;
3. attach the derivation, convergence study, or evidence;
4. list serious alternatives and remaining assumptions;
5. record the date for anything likely to change.
