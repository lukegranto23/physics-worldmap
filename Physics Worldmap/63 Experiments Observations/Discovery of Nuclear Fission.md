# Discovery of Nuclear Fission

> [!question] Scientific question
> What products arise when uranium captures neutrons?

## Method

Chemically identify reaction products and interpret them with nuclear masses and energetics.

## Observable result

Much lighter nuclei such as barium appeared, consistent with the uranium nucleus splitting and releasing large energy.

## What it establishes

Nuclear fission and the possibility of neutron-multiplying chain reactions.

## What it does not establish

Engineering a reactor or weapon requires neutron transport, materials, heat, and kinetics beyond the discovery observation.
