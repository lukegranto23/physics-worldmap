# First Exoplanet Transit Surveys

> [!question] Scientific question
> Can planets be detected from periodic stellar dimming?

## Method

Monitor stellar brightness and search for repeated, shape-consistent dips, then validate against false positives.

## Observable result

Thousands of planetary systems and population trends have been inferred with follow-up confirmation and statistical validation.

## What it establishes

Planet occurrence, radii, orbital periods, and atmospheric opportunities beyond the Solar System.

## What it does not establish

Transit geometry, completeness, stellar parameters, and astrophysical impostors create selection effects.
