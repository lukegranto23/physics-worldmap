# Cosmic Microwave Background Discovery

> [!question] Scientific question
> Does the universe contain relic thermal radiation from a hot early phase?

## Method

Measure excess nearly isotropic microwave antenna temperature and compare across frequencies.

## Observable result

A pervasive approximately 3 K background was found; later measurements established an exquisite blackbody spectrum.

## What it establishes

A hot dense early universe and relic radiation decoupled near recombination.

## What it does not establish

The monopole alone does not determine detailed cosmological parameters or the inflationary mechanism.
