# Fizeau Speed-of-Light Measurement

> [!question] Scientific question
> Can light's finite terrestrial propagation time be measured directly?

## Method

Send light through a rotating toothed wheel to a distant mirror and infer time of flight from extinction conditions.

## Observable result

The inferred speed was finite and close to the modern value.

## What it establishes

Laboratory time-of-flight measurement of light speed.

## What it does not establish

Accuracy is limited by path length, wheel calibration, atmosphere, and geometry.
