# Franck-Hertz Experiment

> [!question] Scientific question
> Do atoms absorb energy continuously or in discrete internal amounts?

## Method

Accelerate electrons through mercury vapor and measure current as a function of voltage.

## Observable result

Repeated current drops correspond to inelastic collisions at characteristic excitation energies.

## What it establishes

Discrete atomic energy levels and collisional excitation.

## What it does not establish

Contact potentials, gas temperature, and multiple collisions complicate the raw voltage spacing.
