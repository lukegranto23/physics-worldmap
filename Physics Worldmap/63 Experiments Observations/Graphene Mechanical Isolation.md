# Graphene Mechanical Isolation

> [!question] Scientific question
> Can a stable one-atom-thick crystal be isolated and electrically characterized?

## Method

Micromechanically exfoliate graphite, identify monolayers, and fabricate field-effect devices.

## Observable result

Monolayer graphene showed ambipolar transport and unusual quantum Hall behavior.

## What it establishes

Stable two-dimensional crystals and a tunable Dirac-like electronic material platform.

## What it does not establish

Mechanical exfoliation is not a complete explanation of graphene's many-body and device behavior.
