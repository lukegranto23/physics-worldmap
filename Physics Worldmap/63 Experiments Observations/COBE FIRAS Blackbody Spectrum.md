# COBE FIRAS Blackbody Spectrum

> [!question] Scientific question
> How close is the cosmic microwave background to a perfect blackbody?

## Method

Use a differential Fourier-transform spectrometer above Earth's atmosphere to compare sky and reference spectra.

## Observable result

The CMB spectrum matched a blackbody with extremely small allowed distortions.

## What it establishes

Directly, FIRAS establishes that the sky-averaged CMB spectrum is extraordinarily close to a blackbody and places stringent limits on spectral distortions. Within hot-Big-Bang models, this supports an early thermal state and constrains later energy injection; the thermalization history is an inference from the spectrum plus the cosmological model, not a separately observed event.

## What it does not establish

A blackbody spectrum alone does not identify the microphysical thermalization process, fix when it occurred, or determine the origin of primordial anisotropies.
