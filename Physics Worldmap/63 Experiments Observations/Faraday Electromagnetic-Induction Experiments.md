# Faraday Electromagnetic-Induction Experiments

> [!question] Scientific question
> Can changing magnetic conditions generate an electric current?

## Method

Change magnetic flux through a circuit using moving magnets, coils, and switching currents.

## Observable result

A transient electromotive force appears with magnitude linked to flux-change rate and direction opposing change.

## What it establishes

Electromagnetic induction and the dynamical relation between electric and magnetic fields.

## What it does not establish

The flux rule is not a complete microscopic model of matter or relativistic field transformation.
