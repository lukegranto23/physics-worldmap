# Aspect Bell-Correlation Experiments

> [!question] Scientific question
> Can local hidden-variable models reproduce polarization correlations of entangled photons?

## Method

Measure correlations at separated polarization analyzers with changing settings and compare a Bell inequality.

## Observable result

The measured correlations violated the tested Bell inequalities and agreed with quantum predictions.

## What it establishes

Local hidden-variable models satisfying the tested Bell assumptions are incompatible with the data.

## What it does not establish

Early tests retained loopholes; Bell violation does not permit faster-than-light signaling or select one quantum interpretation.
