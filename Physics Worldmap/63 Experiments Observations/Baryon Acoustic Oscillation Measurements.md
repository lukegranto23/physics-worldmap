# Baryon Acoustic Oscillation Measurements

> [!question] Scientific question
> Does large-scale clustering retain the sound-horizon scale from the early universe?

## Method

Measure galaxy or tracer correlations across large survey volumes and fit the characteristic separation scale.

## Observable result

A preferred comoving clustering scale consistent with pre-recombination acoustic physics is observed.

## What it establishes

A standard-ruler probe of expansion and strong cross-check of the cosmological model.

## What it does not establish

Distance inference depends on reconstruction, selection, nonlinear clustering, and the sound-horizon calibration model.
