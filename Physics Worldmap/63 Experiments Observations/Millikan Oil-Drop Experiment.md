# Millikan Oil-Drop Experiment

> [!question] Scientific question
> Is electric charge carried in discrete units?

## Method

Balance gravitational, viscous, and electric forces on charged droplets and infer their charge.

## Observable result

Inferred droplet charges clustered near integer multiples of a common elementary charge.

## What it establishes

Charge quantization for ordinary isolated matter and an estimate of the elementary charge.

## What it does not establish

Historical analyses involved selection and calibration issues; modern charge quantization evidence is much broader.
