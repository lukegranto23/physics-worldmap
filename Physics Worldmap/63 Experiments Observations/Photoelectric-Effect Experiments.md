# Photoelectric-Effect Experiments

> [!question] Scientific question
> How does light eject electrons from matter?

## Method

Measure electron stopping potential versus light frequency and intensity.

## Observable result

Electron maximum kinetic energy depends approximately linearly on frequency above a threshold, while intensity mainly changes rate.

## What it establishes

Quantized light-matter energy transfer and the photon relation $E=h\nu$.

## What it does not establish

Material surface physics matters; the observation does not mean light always behaves as classical particles.
