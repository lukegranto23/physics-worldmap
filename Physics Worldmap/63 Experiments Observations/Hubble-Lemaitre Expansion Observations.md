# Hubble-Lemaitre Expansion Observations

> [!question] Scientific question
> Is there a systematic relation between galaxy distance and spectral redshift?

## Method

Combine galaxy redshifts with then-available distance indicators.

## Observable result

More distant galaxies showed larger redshifts on average, interpreted in relativistic cosmology as expansion.

## What it establishes

The empirical redshift-distance relation and evidence for an evolving universe.

## What it does not establish

At low precision peculiar velocities and distance calibration matter; redshift is not simply motion through static space.
