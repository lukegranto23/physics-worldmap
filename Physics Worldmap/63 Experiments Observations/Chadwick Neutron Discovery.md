# Chadwick Neutron Discovery

> [!question] Scientific question
> What neutral penetrating radiation is emitted when beryllium is bombarded by alpha particles?

## Method

Measure recoil energies of light nuclei struck by the radiation.

## Observable result

Kinematics required a neutral particle with mass close to the proton rather than high-energy gamma rays.

## What it establishes

The neutron as a nuclear constituent.

## What it does not establish

It did not reveal neutron quark structure or the modern nuclear force.
