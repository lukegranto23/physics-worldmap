# Higgs-Boson Discovery

> [!question] Scientific question
> Does a new scalar resonance associated with electroweak symmetry breaking exist near the predicted search range?

## Method

Combine multiple decay channels in proton-proton collision data from ATLAS and CMS.

## Observable result

Both experiments observed a new boson near 125 GeV with significance above the discovery threshold and properties compatible with the Standard Model Higgs.

## What it establishes

A Higgs-like scalar and the central mechanism of electroweak mass generation in the tested channels.

## What it does not establish

Current measurements still test rare couplings, self-interaction, and possible non-Standard-Model admixtures.
