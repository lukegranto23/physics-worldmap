# Foucault Pendulum

> [!question] Scientific question
> Can Earth's rotation be demonstrated by a local mechanical trajectory?

## Method

Track the slow precession of a long pendulum's swing plane relative to the floor.

## Observable result

The precession rate varies with latitude as expected for motion in a rotating frame.

## What it establishes

Earth rotates and noninertial Coriolis terms have observable consequences.

## What it does not establish

The ideal formula requires small oscillations and controlled suspension, air drag, and anisotropy.
