# Compton-Scattering Experiment

> [!question] Scientific question
> Does electromagnetic radiation transfer particle-like momentum to electrons?

## Method

Scatter X-rays from weakly bound electrons and measure wavelength shift versus angle.

## Observable result

The shift follows $\Delta\lambda=(h/m_ec)(1-\cos\theta)$.

## What it establishes

Photon energy-momentum and relativistic collision kinematics.

## What it does not establish

Binding and many-body effects modify simple free-electron scattering in materials.
