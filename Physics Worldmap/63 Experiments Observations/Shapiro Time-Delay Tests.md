# Shapiro Time-Delay Tests

> [!question] Scientific question
> Does spacetime curvature delay signals passing near a gravitating mass?

## Method

Time radar or radio signals whose paths pass near the Sun or other masses.

## Observable result

An excess round-trip delay consistent with general relativity is observed.

## What it establishes

Relativistic light propagation in curved spacetime and constraints on post-Newtonian gravity.

## What it does not establish

Solar plasma and ephemeris modeling are important systematics.
