# Stern-Gerlach Experiment

> [!question] Scientific question
> How is atomic angular momentum projected in a magnetic field?

## Method

Pass a beam of neutral silver atoms through a strongly nonuniform magnetic field.

## Observable result

The beam split into two discrete components rather than a classical continuum.

## What it establishes

Space quantization and, in the modern account, spin-one-half behavior of the relevant atomic state.

## What it does not establish

It is not a direct measurement of a bare electron spin independent of the atom and apparatus.
