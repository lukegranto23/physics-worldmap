# National Ignition Facility Ignition Experiments

> [!question] Scientific question
> Can an inertially confined capsule produce fusion yield exceeding the laser energy incident on the target?

## Method

Use a laser-driven hohlraum to compress and heat a deuterium-tritium capsule and infer yield with multiple diagnostics.

## Observable result

Repeated shots achieved target-gain above one under the stated incident-laser-energy definition.

## What it establishes

A burning-plasma and ignition milestone for inertial confinement.

## What it does not establish

Target gain is not facility wall-plug gain, repetition-rate readiness, or a complete power-plant demonstration.
