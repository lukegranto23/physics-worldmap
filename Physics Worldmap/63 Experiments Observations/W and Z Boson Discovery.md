# W and Z Boson Discovery

> [!question] Scientific question
> Do the massive carriers predicted by electroweak theory exist?

## Method

Produce high-energy proton-antiproton collisions and identify leptonic decay signatures with missing momentum or lepton pairs.

## Observable result

Mass peaks and event kinematics consistent with charged W and neutral Z bosons were observed.

## What it establishes

The massive electroweak gauge bosons and quantitative electroweak theory.

## What it does not establish

Discovery did not by itself establish the Higgs mechanism as the unique source of mass.
