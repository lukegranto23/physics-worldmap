# Hulse-Taylor Binary Pulsar

> [!question] Scientific question
> Do compact binaries lose orbital energy at the rate predicted for gravitational radiation?

## Method

Time pulsar pulses over decades and infer orbital-period change after environmental and kinematic corrections.

## Observable result

The orbital decay agrees closely with general-relativistic gravitational-wave energy loss.

## What it establishes

Indirect, precision evidence for gravitational radiation and strong-field binary dynamics.

## What it does not establish

It was not a direct strain detection and relies on binary timing and correction models.
