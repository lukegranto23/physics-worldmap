# Type-Ia Supernova Cosmic-Acceleration Discovery

> [!question] Scientific question
> How has the cosmic expansion rate changed over time?

## Method

Standardize Type-Ia supernova luminosities and compare distance-redshift relations with cosmological models.

## Observable result

Distant supernovae appeared dimmer than expected in decelerating matter-only cosmologies, favoring late-time acceleration.

## What it establishes

Accelerated expansion within a broad relativistic cosmological framework.

## What it does not establish

The observations do not uniquely identify a cosmological constant; calibration, populations, dust, and model assumptions are audited continuously.
