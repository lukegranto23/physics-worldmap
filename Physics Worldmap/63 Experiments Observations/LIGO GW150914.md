# LIGO GW150914

> [!question] Scientific question
> Can spacetime strain from a compact-binary merger be detected directly?

## Method

Cross-correlate calibrated laser-interferometer strain data at separated detectors and compare with relativistic waveforms.

## Observable result

A transient chirp consistent with a stellar-mass binary black-hole merger was observed in both detectors.

## What it establishes

Direct gravitational-wave detection, binary black-hole existence, and strong-field dynamical agreement with general relativity.

## What it does not establish

One event does not exhaust alternative-gravity tests; inference depends on waveform and noise models.
