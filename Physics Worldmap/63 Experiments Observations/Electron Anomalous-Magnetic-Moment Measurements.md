# Electron Anomalous-Magnetic-Moment Measurements

> [!question] Scientific question
> How accurately does quantum electrodynamics predict the electron magnetic moment?

## Method

Confine a single electron, measure cyclotron and spin-precession frequencies, and combine with independent constants.

## Observable result

Experiment and high-order QED calculations agree at extraordinary precision within input-constant and theory uncertainties.

## What it establishes

A stringent quantum-field-theory test and sensitivity to virtual heavy physics.

## What it does not establish

The comparison depends strongly on the independently determined fine-structure constant and on complete theory terms.
