# Positron Discovery in Cosmic Rays

> [!question] Scientific question
> Does the positive-electron solution of relativistic quantum theory correspond to a real particle?

## Method

Track curved charged-particle paths in a magnetic cloud chamber with energy-loss information.

## Observable result

A positively charged particle with electron-like mass was identified.

## What it establishes

Antimatter and a major prediction of the Dirac equation.

## What it does not establish

A single track classification relies on field direction, curvature, and material-interaction evidence.
