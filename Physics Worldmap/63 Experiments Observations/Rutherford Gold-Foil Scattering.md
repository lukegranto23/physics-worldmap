# Rutherford Gold-Foil Scattering

> [!question] Scientific question
> How is positive charge distributed inside atoms?

## Method

Scatter alpha particles through thin metal foil and count angular deflections.

## Observable result

Rare large-angle events were far more common than diffuse-charge models predicted and fit scattering from a compact charged nucleus.

## What it establishes

Atoms contain a tiny massive nucleus and scattering can image substructure.

## What it does not establish

It did not reveal neutrons, quantum chromodynamics, or detailed nuclear structure.
