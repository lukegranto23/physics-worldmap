# Hertzsprung-Russell Diagram

> [!question] Scientific question
> How are stellar luminosity and temperature distributed?

## Method

Combine distance-corrected brightness with spectral type or effective temperature for stellar populations.

## Observable result

Stars occupy structured sequences and branches rather than filling the diagram uniformly.

## What it establishes

Stellar radii, masses, lifetimes, and evolutionary phases form a coherent physical sequence.

## What it does not establish

A location on the diagram can be degenerate in composition, age, rotation, and multiplicity.
