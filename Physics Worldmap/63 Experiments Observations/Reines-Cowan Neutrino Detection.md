# Reines-Cowan Neutrino Detection

> [!question] Scientific question
> Can reactor antineutrinos be detected directly?

## Method

Observe time-correlated inverse-beta-decay positron and neutron signals near a reactor.

## Observable result

An excess of correlated events appeared with the reactor source as expected.

## What it establishes

Direct neutrino interaction detection and the reality of the neutrino as a particle.

## What it does not establish

It did not measure flavor mixing or neutrino mass.
