# Hall-Effect Experiment

> [!question] Scientific question
> What transverse response arises when current-carrying matter is placed in a magnetic field?

## Method

Drive current through a conductor and measure transverse voltage under perpendicular magnetic field.

## Observable result

A signed Hall voltage scales with field and current in a simple regime.

## What it establishes

Carrier sign and density in elementary transport models and transverse electromagnetic response.

## What it does not establish

Multiband, anomalous, and quantum Hall effects require richer descriptions.
