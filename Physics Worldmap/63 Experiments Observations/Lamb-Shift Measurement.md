# Lamb-Shift Measurement

> [!question] Scientific question
> Are hydrogen levels predicted degenerate by the simplest Dirac-Coulomb theory actually identical?

## Method

Use microwave spectroscopy to compare closely spaced hydrogen states.

## Observable result

A small splitting was observed and quantitatively explained by radiative QED corrections.

## What it establishes

Vacuum fluctuations, self-energy, and renormalized quantum electrodynamics have measurable atomic effects.

## What it does not establish

The phrase vacuum fluctuation is interpretive shorthand; precision calculation includes several controlled contributions.
