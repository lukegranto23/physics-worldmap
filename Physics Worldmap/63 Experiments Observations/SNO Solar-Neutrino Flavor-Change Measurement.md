# SNO Solar-Neutrino Flavor-Change Measurement

> [!question] Scientific question
> Are solar electron neutrinos disappearing or changing flavor?

## Method

Use heavy water to compare charged-current electron-neutrino events with neutral-current all-active-flavor events.

## Observable result

The total active-neutrino flux agreed with solar models while the electron flavor was depleted.

## What it establishes

Solar neutrino flavor transformation and strong resolution of the solar-neutrino problem.

## What it does not establish

It does not determine whether additional sterile components exist at all parameter values.
