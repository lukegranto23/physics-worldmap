# Ramsey Separated-Oscillatory-Fields Method

> [!question] Scientific question
> How can transition frequencies be measured with narrow interference fringes?

## Method

Apply two coherent interaction zones separated by free phase evolution and scan detuning.

## Observable result

The population shows Ramsey fringes with width set mainly by the separation time.

## What it establishes

Coherent phase accumulation and a foundation of atomic clocks and precision spectroscopy.

## What it does not establish

Systematic phase shifts, decoherence, and pulse imperfections limit accuracy.
