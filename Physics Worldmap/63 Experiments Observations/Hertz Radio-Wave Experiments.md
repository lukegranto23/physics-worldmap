# Hertz Radio-Wave Experiments

> [!question] Scientific question
> Do Maxwell's equations describe propagating electromagnetic waves?

## Method

Generate sparks in a transmitting circuit and detect resonant sparks, reflection, interference, and polarization remotely.

## Observable result

The detected radiation displayed wave behavior and a propagation speed consistent with light.

## What it establishes

Radio waves are electromagnetic and Maxwell's synthesis has direct experimental consequences.

## What it does not establish

The early apparatus did not measure all modern precision properties of Maxwell theory.
