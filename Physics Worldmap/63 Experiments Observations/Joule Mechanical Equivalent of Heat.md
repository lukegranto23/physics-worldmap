# Joule Mechanical Equivalent of Heat

> [!question] Scientific question
> Are mechanical work and heat quantitatively interchangeable forms of energy transfer?

## Method

Convert falling-weight work into fluid stirring and measure the temperature rise with careful calorimetry.

## Observable result

A reproducible proportionality links mechanical work to thermal energy.

## What it establishes

Energy conservation unifies mechanical and thermal processes and supports the first law.

## What it does not establish

It does not provide a microscopic explanation of temperature or entropy.
