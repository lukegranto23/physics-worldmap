# Galileo Inclined-Plane Experiments

> [!question] Scientific question
> How does a freely falling body accelerate?

## Method

Slow gravitational acceleration with inclined planes and compare distances traversed in equal times.

## Observable result

Distance grows approximately as time squared for uniform acceleration, and the acceleration is not proportional to ordinary object weight.

## What it establishes

The kinematics of nearly uniform gravitational acceleration and the power of controlled time-distance measurement.

## What it does not establish

The exact modern reconstruction of Galileo's procedures is historical scholarship; the experiment does not measure relativistic corrections.
