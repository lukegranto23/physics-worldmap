# Gravity Probe B

> [!question] Scientific question
> Do orbiting gyroscopes precess due to spacetime curvature and Earth's rotation?

## Method

Track cryogenic gyroscope spin directions relative to a guide star in polar orbit.

## Observable result

Geodetic and frame-dragging precession estimates were consistent with general-relativistic predictions within reported uncertainties.

## What it establishes

Independent weak-field constraints on relativistic precession.

## What it does not establish

Complex instrument torques and calibration dominated parts of the analysis.
