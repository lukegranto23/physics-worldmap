# Loophole-Free Bell Tests

> [!question] Scientific question
> Do Bell violations persist when major detection and locality loopholes are closed together?

## Method

Use high-efficiency entangled systems, spacelike-separated setting choices, and event-ready or photonic protocols.

## Observable result

Multiple experiments rejected relevant local-realist null models while closing major loopholes under stated assumptions.

## What it establishes

The robustness of Bell nonlocal correlations against the principal experimental loopholes.

## What it does not establish

No finite experiment eliminates every philosophical assumption such as measurement independence.
