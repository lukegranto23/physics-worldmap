# Michelson Interferometer

> [!question] Scientific question
> How can tiny optical path differences be measured?

## Method

Split coherent light into perpendicular paths, recombine it, and monitor phase-dependent fringes.

## Observable result

Sub-wavelength path changes produce measurable fringe shifts.

## What it establishes

Interferometry as a precision transducer for length, refractive index, time, and strain.

## What it does not establish

Interpretation depends on calibration and environmental noise; the device alone is not a specific relativity test.
