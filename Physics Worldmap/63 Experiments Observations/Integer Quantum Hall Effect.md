# Integer Quantum Hall Effect

> [!question] Scientific question
> How does a two-dimensional electron gas conduct in strong magnetic field at low temperature?

## Method

Measure longitudinal and transverse resistance while sweeping magnetic field or carrier density.

## Observable result

Hall resistance forms extraordinarily precise plateaus $R_{xy}=h/(\nu e^2)$ while longitudinal resistance vanishes near plateau centers.

## What it establishes

Topological quantization, edge transport, and a resistance standard.

## What it does not establish

Interactions and disorder help plateau formation; the integer effect is not the whole fractional Hall story.
