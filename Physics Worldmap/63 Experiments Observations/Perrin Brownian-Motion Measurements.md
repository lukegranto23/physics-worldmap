# Perrin Brownian-Motion Measurements

> [!question] Scientific question
> Can thermal molecular motion and atomic-scale constants be inferred from visible colloids?

## Method

Measure sedimentation profiles and Brownian displacements of calibrated colloidal particles.

## Observable result

Independent methods yielded consistent estimates of Avogadro's number and diffusion behavior.

## What it establishes

Quantitative atomism and the statistical theory of Brownian motion.

## What it does not establish

The analysis assumes a suitable colloid model, thermal equilibrium, and controlled hydrodynamics.
