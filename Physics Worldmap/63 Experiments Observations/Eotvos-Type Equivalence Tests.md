# Eotvos-Type Equivalence Tests

> [!question] Scientific question
> Do different compositions fall with identical acceleration?

## Method

Use torsion balances or free-fall instruments to compare differential acceleration toward an attractor.

## Observable result

No composition-dependent acceleration has been established within increasingly tight sensitivity.

## What it establishes

The weak equivalence principle is highly constrained in tested compositions, fields, and ranges.

## What it does not establish

A null result does not prove exact universality at every range or for every form of matter.
