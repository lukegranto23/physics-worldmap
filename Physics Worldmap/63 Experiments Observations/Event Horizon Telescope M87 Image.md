# Event Horizon Telescope M87 Image

> [!question] Scientific question
> Can horizon-scale structure around a supermassive black hole be resolved?

## Method

Combine millimeter telescopes with very-long-baseline interferometry and reconstruct images under multiple pipelines.

## Observable result

A bright asymmetric ring with diameter consistent with the lensed emission region around a Kerr-like black hole was recovered.

## What it establishes

Horizon-scale compact structure and strong-gravity consistency for M87's central object.

## What it does not establish

The image is not a photograph of the event horizon itself and depends on sparse interferometric reconstruction and emission models.
