# Pulsar Discovery

> [!question] Scientific question
> What produces highly regular radio pulses from the sky?

## Method

Search radio time series for dispersed periodic signals and track timing stability.

## Observable result

Rapid periodic pulses with interstellar dispersion were found and identified with rotating compact stars.

## What it establishes

Neutron stars exist and can act as precise rotating clocks.

## What it does not establish

Pulse emission physics and magnetospheric details remain active research.
