# Kennedy-Thorndike Experiment

> [!question] Scientific question
> Does light-speed isotropy remain consistent when laboratory velocity changes with time and interferometer arms are unequal?

## Method

Use unequal optical paths and search for velocity-dependent phase changes as Earth moves.

## Observable result

No signal consistent with a preferred-frame velocity dependence was established.

## What it establishes

A complementary Lorentz-invariance constraint distinct from Michelson-Morley.

## What it does not establish

Results are framework-dependent bounds on classes of violations, not proof of every Lorentz-invariant extension.
