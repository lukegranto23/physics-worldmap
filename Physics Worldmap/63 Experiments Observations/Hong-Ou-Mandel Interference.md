# Hong-Ou-Mandel Interference

> [!question] Scientific question
> How do two indistinguishable photons interfere at a beamsplitter?

## Method

Vary the relative arrival time of two single photons entering opposite beamsplitter ports and count coincidences.

## Observable result

Coincidence counts dip near simultaneous arrival because two-photon amplitudes interfere.

## What it establishes

Bosonic indistinguishability, two-photon interference, and a tool for photon-source characterization.

## What it does not establish

A dip can be degraded by distinguishability and multiphoton backgrounds and requires a full measurement model.
