# Meissner-Ochsenfeld Experiment

> [!question] Scientific question
> Is a superconductor merely a perfect conductor or a distinct thermodynamic phase?

## Method

Cool a material through its transition in an applied magnetic field and map expelled flux.

## Observable result

Magnetic flux is expelled from the bulk below the critical field, subject to penetration and geometry.

## What it establishes

The Meissner effect distinguishes superconductivity from ideal zero resistance alone.

## What it does not establish

Type-II superconductors admit vortices over part of their phase diagram.
