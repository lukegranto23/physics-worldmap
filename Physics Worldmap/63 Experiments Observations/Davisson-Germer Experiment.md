# Davisson-Germer Experiment

> [!question] Scientific question
> Do electrons exhibit a de Broglie wavelength?

## Method

Scatter an electron beam from a crystal and measure intensity versus angle and energy.

## Observable result

Diffraction peaks obey crystal interference with wavelength $h/p$.

## What it establishes

Matter-wave interference and quantum momentum-wavelength duality.

## What it does not establish

The crystal potential and multiple scattering require more detail than a simple two-slit analogy.
