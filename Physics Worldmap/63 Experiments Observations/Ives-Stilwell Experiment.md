# Ives-Stilwell Experiment

> [!question] Scientific question
> Does the relativistic transverse Doppler effect follow time dilation?

## Method

Measure forward and backward Doppler-shifted spectral lines from fast ions.

## Observable result

The symmetric combination reveals the second-order frequency shift predicted by special relativity.

## What it establishes

Relativistic time dilation and energy-frequency transformation for moving emitters.

## What it does not establish

The test probes specified boost kinematics and must control beam speed and line systematics.
