# Oersted Current-Compass Experiment

> [!question] Scientific question
> Does electric current affect magnetism?

## Method

Place a compass near a current-carrying wire and reverse the current direction.

## Observable result

The compass deflects with an orientation that reverses with current.

## What it establishes

Electric currents generate magnetic fields and electricity and magnetism are coupled.

## What it does not establish

It does not determine the full field law or displacement current.
