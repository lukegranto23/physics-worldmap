# Tokamak Magnetic-Confinement Experiments

> [!question] Scientific question
> Can a hot plasma be confined long enough for fusion-relevant conditions?

## Method

Drive and shape a toroidal plasma, heat it, and diagnose density, temperature, current, stability, and energy confinement.

## Observable result

Tokamaks achieved progressively higher fusion performance and controlled many macroscopic modes, while steady, disruption-resilient reactor operation remains an engineering and physics frontier.

## What it establishes

Magnetic confinement is viable and quantitatively described by a combination of MHD, kinetic, and empirical transport knowledge.

## What it does not establish

High plasma gain is not the same as net electric power or economic plant performance.
