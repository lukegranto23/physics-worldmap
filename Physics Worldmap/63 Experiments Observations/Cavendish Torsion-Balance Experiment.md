# Cavendish Torsion-Balance Experiment

> [!question] Scientific question
> How strong is the gravitational attraction between laboratory masses?

## Method

Measure the twist of a calibrated torsion fiber caused by nearby source masses.

## Observable result

A finite inverse-square attraction consistent with Newtonian gravity allowed the mean density of Earth, and later G, to be inferred.

## What it establishes

Gravity acts measurably between laboratory bodies and has a very small coupling strength.

## What it does not establish

It does not by itself test strong gravity, gravitational radiation, or quantum properties of gravity.
