# Atmospheric Muon Lifetime Test

> [!question] Scientific question
> Why do unstable muons created high in the atmosphere reach sea level in large numbers?

## Method

Compare decay lifetime, flux versus altitude, and relativistic energy of cosmic-ray muons.

## Observable result

Survival is consistent with proper-time decay and Lorentz time dilation.

## What it establishes

Relativistic clock behavior for unstable particles and consistency of frame descriptions.

## What it does not establish

Atmospheric production and detector acceptance must be modeled; it is not a gravity test.
