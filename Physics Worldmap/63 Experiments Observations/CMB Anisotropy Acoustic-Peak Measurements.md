# CMB Anisotropy Acoustic-Peak Measurements

> [!question] Scientific question
> What initial fluctuations and cosmic contents produced microwave-background temperature and polarization patterns?

## Method

Map calibrated multi-frequency sky anisotropies and infer angular power spectra while separating foregrounds.

## Observable result

A sequence of acoustic peaks and polarization correlations fits a nearly flat, adiabatic, cold-dark-matter cosmology with tightly constrained parameters.

## What it establishes

Coherent primordial perturbations, recombination physics, and a quantitatively successful standard cosmological model.

## What it does not establish

Parameter inference is conditional on a model and foreground, beam, calibration, and likelihood assumptions.
