# Deep-Inelastic Electron Scattering

> [!question] Scientific question
> Do protons contain pointlike momentum-carrying constituents?

## Method

Scatter high-energy electrons from nucleons at large momentum transfer and measure inclusive structure functions.

## Observable result

Approximate scaling and angular-energy distributions revealed pointlike partons, later identified with quarks and gluons.

## What it establishes

Substructure of hadrons and a central empirical basis for QCD.

## What it does not establish

Parton distributions are scale-dependent quantum objects, not fixed classical particles inside a proton.
