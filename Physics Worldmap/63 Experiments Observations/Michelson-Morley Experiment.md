# Michelson-Morley Experiment

> [!question] Scientific question
> Is there a direction-dependent light speed caused by motion through a luminiferous ether?

## Method

Rotate an orthogonal-arm interferometer and search for periodic fringe shifts.

## Observable result

The expected classical ether-wind signal was not observed at the predicted magnitude.

## What it establishes

Strong constraints on simple stationary-ether models and a key empirical route toward Lorentz symmetry.

## What it does not establish

One historical null experiment alone is not the full evidence for special relativity.
