# Super-Kamiokande Atmospheric-Neutrino Oscillations

> [!question] Scientific question
> Why does the atmospheric muon-neutrino rate depend on zenith angle and path length?

## Method

Classify large water-Cherenkov events by flavor proxy, energy, and direction.

## Observable result

Upward-going muon-neutrino-like events were depleted in a pattern consistent with oscillations.

## What it establishes

Neutrino flavor change and therefore nonzero neutrino mass differences.

## What it does not establish

Oscillations measure mass-squared differences and mixing, not the absolute mass scale.
