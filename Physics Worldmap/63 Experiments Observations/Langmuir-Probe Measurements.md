# Langmuir-Probe Measurements

> [!question] Scientific question
> How can local plasma potential, density, and electron temperature be inferred?

## Method

Sweep the voltage of an inserted electrode and measure collected current.

## Observable result

Characteristic current-voltage regions encode ion saturation, electron retardation, and electron collection under model assumptions.

## What it establishes

A foundational local plasma diagnostic and evidence for nontrivial distribution functions.

## What it does not establish

Sheaths, magnetic fields, non-Maxwellian distributions, contamination, and perturbation of the plasma complicate inference.
