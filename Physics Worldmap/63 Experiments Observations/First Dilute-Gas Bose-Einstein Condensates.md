# First Dilute-Gas Bose-Einstein Condensates

> [!question] Scientific question
> Can a weakly interacting atomic gas macroscopically occupy one quantum state?

## Method

Laser-cool and evaporatively cool trapped alkali atoms, then image momentum or density distributions.

## Observable result

A sharp low-momentum component and coherent matter-wave behavior appeared below a critical temperature.

## What it establishes

Bose-Einstein condensation in dilute trapped atomic gases and tunable macroscopic quantum matter.

## What it does not establish

Finite size, trapping, and interactions modify the homogeneous ideal-gas model.
