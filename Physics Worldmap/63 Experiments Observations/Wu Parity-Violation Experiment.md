# Wu Parity-Violation Experiment

> [!question] Scientific question
> Is mirror symmetry conserved in weak beta decay?

## Method

Polarize cobalt-60 nuclei at low temperature and measure electron emission relative to nuclear spin.

## Observable result

Electrons were emitted preferentially opposite the spin direction, violating parity symmetry.

## What it establishes

The weak interaction is not invariant under spatial inversion.

## What it does not establish

It does not imply that all interactions violate parity or by itself establish the full V-A theory.
