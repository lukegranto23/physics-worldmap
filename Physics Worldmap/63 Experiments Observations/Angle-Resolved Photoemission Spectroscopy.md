# Angle-Resolved Photoemission Spectroscopy

> [!question] Scientific question
> How can occupied electronic dispersion and spectral weight be measured?

## Method

Eject electrons with photons and measure their kinetic energy and emission angle.

## Observable result

Under a controlled sudden-approximation analysis, intensity maps occupied band energies, lifetimes, and matrix-element-weighted spectral functions.

## What it establishes

Direct empirical access to momentum-resolved electronic structure.

## What it does not establish

Surface sensitivity, matrix elements, resolution, and many-body interpretation prevent a raw image from being the band structure itself.
