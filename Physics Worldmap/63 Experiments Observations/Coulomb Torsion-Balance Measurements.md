# Coulomb Torsion-Balance Measurements

> [!question] Scientific question
> How does electrostatic force depend on charge separation?

## Method

Use a torsion balance to infer weak forces between charged bodies at controlled distances.

## Observable result

The force is consistent with an inverse-square distance law in the tested regime.

## What it establishes

The quantitative electrostatic force law and the utility of null or torsion methods.

## What it does not establish

Macroscopic charge leakage and geometry require modeling; modern tests extend precision and range.
