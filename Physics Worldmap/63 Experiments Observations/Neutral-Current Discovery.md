# Neutral-Current Discovery

> [!question] Scientific question
> Do weak interactions occur without exchanging electric charge?

## Method

Observe neutrino events in which hadrons recoil but no charged lepton appears.

## Observable result

Neutral-current event candidates were found at rates compatible with electroweak theory.

## What it establishes

Weak neutral currents and a key prediction of electroweak unification.

## What it does not establish

Background separation and cross-section modeling were central to the inference.
