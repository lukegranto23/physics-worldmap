# GW170817 Multimessenger Neutron-Star Merger

> [!question] Scientific question
> What can joint gravitational and electromagnetic signals reveal about a binary neutron-star merger?

## Method

Combine gravitational-wave strain, gamma-ray, optical, infrared, X-ray, and radio observations.

## Observable result

A consistent source enabled constraints on propagation speed, binary parameters, kilonova ejecta, and heavy-element production.

## What it establishes

Binary neutron-star mergers produce gravitational waves and electromagnetic counterparts and contribute to r-process nucleosynthesis.

## What it does not establish

The event does not fix all merger rates, equations of state, or nucleosynthetic yields.
