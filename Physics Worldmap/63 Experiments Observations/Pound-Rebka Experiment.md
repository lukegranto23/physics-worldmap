# Pound-Rebka Experiment

> [!question] Scientific question
> Do clocks and photon frequencies shift with gravitational potential?

## Method

Use recoil-free gamma-ray resonance across a vertical height and compensate the tiny frequency shift by controlled motion.

## Observable result

The measured shift agreed with gravitational redshift in Earth's weak field.

## What it establishes

The equivalence principle and metric gravitational time dilation in a laboratory regime.

## What it does not establish

It is a weak-field test and does not uniquely determine the full nonlinear field equations.
