# Young Double-Slit Experiment

> [!question] Scientific question
> How do coherent alternatives combine?

## Method

Illuminate two narrow apertures and record the spatial intensity pattern downstream.

## Observable result

Alternating bright and dark fringes occur where amplitudes add constructively or destructively.

## What it establishes

Wave superposition and phase-sensitive interference; single-particle versions demonstrate quantum amplitude interference.

## What it does not establish

A fringe pattern alone does not select an interpretation of quantum mechanics.
