# Condensed Matter Physics Map

> [!abstract]
> Collective quantum and classical behavior of solids, liquids, low-dimensional systems, and engineered materials.

## Questions that organize the field

- How do symmetry, interactions, topology, and disorder determine phases?
- Which quasiparticles and effective fields describe low-energy behavior?
- How do materials conduct, magnetize, superconduct, and transform?
- How can new phases be predicted, synthesized, and controlled?

## Working principles

- Start from symmetry, dimensionality, filling, interactions, and relevant energy scales.
- Low-energy quasiparticles need not resemble microscopic constituents.
- Distinguish band, interaction, disorder, and finite-temperature effects.
- Connect theory to specific probes and material constraints.

## Canonical mathematical handles

- **Bloch theorem:** $\psi_{n\mathbf k}=e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}u_{n\mathbf k}$ — Periodic lattices organize states into bands.
- **Fermi distribution:** $f(E)=[e^{(E-\mu)/k_BT}+1]^{-1}$ — Fermionic occupations form a Fermi surface at low T.
- **BCS gap:** $\Delta\sim2\hbar\omega_D e^{-1/N(0)V}$ — Weak attraction destabilizes the Fermi surface to pairing.
- **Hall conductance:** $\sigma_{xy}=\nu e^2/h$ — Two-dimensional Hall response can be quantized.

## Concept atlas

| Concept | Level | Status | One-line orientation |
|---|---:|---|---|
| [[Crystal Lattices]] | introductory | established | Periodic arrangements are classified by Bravais lattices, bases, space groups, and reciprocal structure. |
| [[Reciprocal Lattice]] | intermediate | established | The reciprocal lattice organizes diffraction and crystal momentum for a periodic structure. |
| [[Brillouin Zones]] | intermediate | established | A Brillouin zone is a primitive cell of reciprocal space whose high-symmetry regions organize band structure. |
| [[X Ray and Neutron Diffraction]] | intermediate | established | Elastic scattering peaks reveal periodic structure through momentum transfer matching reciprocal vectors. |
| [[Phonons]] | intermediate | established | Quantized normal modes of lattice vibration carry energy, momentum, heat, and mediate interactions. |
| [[Debye Model]] | intermediate | established | A linear acoustic spectrum with a cutoff captures the low-temperature lattice heat capacity. |
| [[Bloch Theorem]] | intermediate | established | Single-particle eigenstates in a periodic potential are plane waves modulated by lattice-periodic functions. |
| [[Electronic Band Structure]] | intermediate | established | Hybridization and periodicity form allowed energy bands whose filling and gaps shape material behavior. |
| [[Metals Insulators and Semiconductors]] | introductory | established | Filling, band gaps, interactions, disorder, and topology distinguish conducting regimes. |
| [[Effective Mass]] | intermediate | established | Band curvature determines a quasiparticle's inertial response to weak forces. |
| [[Fermi Surface]] | intermediate | established | At zero temperature, a metal's occupied single-particle states end at a momentum-space surface controlling low-energy response. |
| [[Drude Model]] | introductory | effective | A classical relaxation-time model explains basic electrical and thermal transport while missing quantum band structure. |
| [[Boltzmann Transport in Solids]] | advanced | established | Semiclassical quasiparticle distributions evolve under fields, gradients, and collisions to produce transport coefficients. |
| [[Magnetism in Matter]] | intermediate | established | Orbital motion, spin, exchange, anisotropy, and collective ordering produce diverse magnetic phases. |
| [[Ferromagnetism]] | intermediate | established | Exchange favors aligned moments and spontaneous magnetization below a transition temperature. |
| [[Antiferromagnetism]] | intermediate | established | Exchange favors staggered order with zero or small net magnetization and collective spin waves. |
| [[Spin Waves and Magnons]] | advanced | established | Small collective rotations of ordered moments propagate as waves whose quanta are magnons. |
| [[Superconductivity]] | intermediate | established | A superconducting phase has zero DC resistance, magnetic flux expulsion, phase coherence, and a gap or stiffness. |
| [[BCS Theory]] | advanced | established | An arbitrarily weak attractive interaction near a Fermi surface can form Cooper pairs and a coherent gapped state. |
| [[Ginzburg Landau Theory]] | advanced | established | A complex order parameter and gauge field describe superconductivity near the transition phenomenologically. |
| [[Josephson Effects]] | advanced | established | Phase differences across a weak link drive supercurrent and voltage-controlled phase evolution. |
| [[Quantum Hall Effects]] | advanced | established | Two-dimensional electrons in strong magnetic fields exhibit precisely quantized Hall conductance and edge transport. |
| [[Berry Curvature in Solids]] | advanced | established | Geometric structure of Bloch states modifies semiclassical dynamics and integrates to topological invariants. |
| [[Topological Insulators]] | advanced | established | A bulk band topology protected by symmetry enforces robust conducting boundary states. |
| [[Topological Order]] | advanced | active | Long-range entanglement, fractional excitations, and topology-dependent degeneracy characterize phases beyond local symmetry breaking. |
| [[Strongly Correlated Electrons]] | advanced | active | When interactions compete with kinetic energy, independent-particle pictures fail and new collective phases emerge. |
| [[Hubbard Model]] | advanced | active | A minimal lattice model balances hopping and onsite interaction, capturing Mott physics and magnetic exchange. |
| [[Mott Insulators]] | advanced | established | Strong repulsion can localize electrons at fillings where band theory predicts a metal. |
| [[Fermi Liquid Theory]] | advanced | established | Interacting fermions can retain long-lived quasiparticles adiabatically connected to a free Fermi gas. |
| [[Non Fermi Liquids]] | advanced | active | Non-Fermi-liquid behavior is established in several systems and controlled in selected models, while its microscopic origin and universality in major material classes remain active questions. |
| [[Quantum Phase Transitions]] | advanced | established | Zero-temperature transitions are driven by nonthermal parameters and governed by quantum fluctuations with dynamic scaling. |
| [[Disorder and Anderson Localization]] | advanced | established | Coherent multiple scattering from quenched disorder can localize noninteracting wavefunctions. |
| [[Two Dimensional Materials]] | intermediate | active | Atomically thin materials exhibit strong confinement, tunable bands, valley physics, and enhanced interactions. |
| [[Moire Quantum Matter]] | advanced | active | Long-wavelength interference between lattices creates narrow bands and tunable correlated phases. |
| [[Frustrated Magnets]] | advanced | established | Competing local preferences cannot all be satisfied; unusual order, degeneracy, or spin-liquid phases can result, but are not guaranteed. |
| [[Quantum Spin Liquids]] | advanced | active | Interacting spin phases beyond conventional magnetic order; gapped, gapless, and chiral varieties require distinct diagnostics. |
| [[SYK Model]] | advanced | mixed | Large-N random-interaction model with controlled infrared structure; its Schwarzian sector connects to nearly-AdS2 gravity under stated limits. |
