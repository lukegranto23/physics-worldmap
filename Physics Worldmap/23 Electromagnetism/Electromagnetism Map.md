## Canonical mathematical handles

- **Gauss electric:** $\nabla\cdot\mathbf E=\rho/\epsilon_0$ — Charge sources electric flux.
- **Faraday:** $\nabla\times\mathbf E=-\partial_t\mathbf B$ — Changing magnetic flux induces circulation of E.
- **Gauss magnetic:** $\nabla\cdot\mathbf B=0$ — No magnetic monopoles have been observed.
- **Ampere-Maxwell:** $\nabla\times\mathbf B=\mu_0\mathbf J+\mu_0\epsilon_0\partial_t\mathbf E$ — Current and displacement current source B.
