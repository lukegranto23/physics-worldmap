## High-level unsolved bridges

1. A tested quantum description of dynamical spacetime.
2. The microscopic identities of dark matter and dark energy.
3. The origin of the cosmic matter-antimatter asymmetry.
4. The mechanism of neutrino mass and whether neutrinos are Majorana.
5. Predictive principles for far-from-equilibrium many-body organization.
6. Controlled solutions of strongly coupled quantum matter and nuclear QCD.
7. Predictive turbulence across realistic geometries and scales.
8. Physical principles linking molecular dynamics to robust living function.

## What “solved” must mean

- a precise question has an agreed operational target;
- a model predicts that target with controlled approximation and uncertainty;
- independent evidence excludes serious alternatives in the tested regime;
- the result survives calibration, selection, and robustness audits;
- remaining domain limitations are explicit.
